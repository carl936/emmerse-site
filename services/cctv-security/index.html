A CCTV system is most useful when it is planned around real movement through the property. Entrances, access points, blind spots, the height and angle of each camera, and changes between daylight and night conditions all affect what a camera may capture.

For businesses, priorities may include customer entrances, work areas and the location of monitoring equipment. Building and property managers may need to consider shared access areas. We also welcome suitable home CCTV enquiries.

Preparing for a quote

Include the suburb, property type, the areas you want to cover, the approximate number of camera positions you are considering, and any photos or plans available. This makes the initial discussion more useful without locking the job into equipment that has not been assessed.

Privacy is part of responsible placement

Camera positions should be considered carefully so the system focuses on the intended property areas. Owners and operators remain responsible for using their security system appropriately and for understanding any obligations that apply to their circumstances.
