What an EV charger installation involves.

Electric vehicle chargers place an ongoing electrical load on a property. Before installation, the selected charger, the property's electrical capacity, protective equipment, cable route, and mounting position need to be considered together.

For a home, the most practical location is usually driven by where the vehicle is parked and how the charger will be used each day. A business may also need to think about who will use the charging point and how its position affects parking and access.

Before requesting a quote

Useful details include your suburb, whether the site is a home or business, the vehicle or charger you are considering, where the vehicle parks, and any photos showing the proposed area. If you have already selected equipment, include its details without assuming that every charger suits every property.
