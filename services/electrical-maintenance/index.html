An electrician for the next job, too.

When you manage a business or building, electrical issues add to an already busy day. Emmerse can help with lighting faults, power-point repairs, recurring electrical problems and practical attention to existing installations.

We’re available to support property managers, facilities managers, hotels, hospitality venues and apartment building management. Tell us about your premises, access arrangements and the work that needs attention so we can discuss a practical way forward.

Planned and responsive service work

Not every maintenance job is an emergency. Regular business hours are Monday to Saturday, 7:00am to 5:00pm. Free quotes are available for planned work, and emergency electrical callouts remain available 24/7 when an urgent issue cannot wait.

Information that helps when you get in touch

When requesting a quote, include the suburb, the type of property, a short description of the issue, and any relevant photos you can attach in your email. Include any recurring issues, site access arrangements and your preferred timing so we can discuss the next step.

Need urgent help?

Emergency electrical callouts are available 24/7. The typical response time is around 1 hour.
