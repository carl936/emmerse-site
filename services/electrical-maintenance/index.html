Maintenance that keeps things moving.

Small electrical problems can interrupt a home, workplace, or customer-facing business. A maintenance visit may be appropriate when lights or power points stop working correctly, an electrical fault keeps returning, or an existing installation needs practical attention.

Emmerse Electrical works across Metro Melbourne and approaches service work with the same focus on clear communication, careful workmanship, and a tidy result shown throughout the existing project portfolio.

Planned and responsive service work

Not every maintenance job is an emergency. Regular business hours are Monday to Saturday, 7:00am to 5:00pm. Free quotes are available for planned work, and emergency electrical callouts remain available 24/7 when an urgent issue cannot wait.
