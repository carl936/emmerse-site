Privacy

A plain-language summary of what this website does with information.

About this website

This website is operated for Emmerse Electrical, ABN 51 737 303 613. It provides business information, service details, contact links, and a client-side quote email tool.

Analytics and cookies

This website does not currently use Google Analytics, advertising pixels, or marketing cookies. No analytics identifier has been added to the site.

The website is hosted as a static site. Hosting and network providers may process standard technical request information needed to deliver and protect the website, such as an IP address, browser details, requested page, and request time.

Quote email tool

The free quote page runs in your browser. When you enter your name, phone number, suburb, service, and job description, the website uses that information only to prepare a draft email. Selecting “Open Email App” opens your own email application. The website does not store the form contents or send them to a form provider.

Your information is sent to Emmerse Electrical only if you choose to send the email from your email application.

Direct enquiries

If you call or email Emmerse Electrical, the information you provide may be used to respond to your enquiry, prepare a quote, arrange electrical work, and maintain normal business or legal records. Information is kept only for as long as it is reasonably needed for those purposes.

External links

The website links to external services such as Instagram and Google Maps. If you follow an external link, that service operates under its own privacy terms and may collect information independently of this website.

Your privacy questions

For a privacy question about an enquiry you have sent to Emmerse Electrical, email jack@emmerse.au or call 0410 265 528.
